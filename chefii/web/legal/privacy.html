Chefii helps you keep track of the food in your kitchen, decide what to cook, cook it, and understand your nutrition. This policy explains what personal information Chefii collects, why, where it is stored, who processes it, and the choices you have. Chefii is operated by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("we"), [ADDRESS], Canada.

In short

  • We collect what the app needs to work: your account, your kitchen, and the records you choose to keep.
  • Your health and journal records are private to you — other members of your household cannot see them.
  • AI features run only after you turn them on. We never sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • Your data is stored in Canada (Montréal). Some AI requests are processed by providers outside Canada (see below).
  • You can export or delete your data at any time from the app.

We do not collect your location, contacts, payment card details or advertising identifiers, and Chefii contains no advertising.

Consent

We ask for your consent when you create your account and again, separately, before the first time any AI feature processes your content. Health and journal features are optional. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app (You › Privacy & data); some features will then stop working. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened.

How AI is used

When AI processing is on, Chefii sends only what a request needs to an AI provider:

  • Receipt and fridge photos are sent to Google's Gemini API to read the items, for that request only.
  • Recipe suggestions and the chef chat send a list of the food you have, your household's dietary constraints, and your message. They do not include your name, email, journal or health records.
  • If enabled by us and allowed in your settings, conversation features may use OpenAI as an alternative provider. Photos are never sent to OpenAI. You can turn this off in Privacy & data.

We use these providers under terms that do not allow them to train their models on your content. [CONFIRM WITH CURRENT PROVIDER TERMS.] AI output is checked by Chefii before you see it — for example, recipes are screened again for your allergies — but AI can make mistakes, so please review what it suggests.

Who can see your information

  • Your household: members share the kitchen, receipts, meal plan and shopping list. Your health and journal records are not shared. You may choose to share your allergies with your household so recipes are screened for everyone.
  • Professionals you invite: you can give a verified professional (for example a registered dietitian) read-only access to categories you choose, for a period you choose. You can revoke access at any time, and you can see when they viewed your records.
  • Us: our staff access personal information only when necessary to operate, secure or support the service, and that access is logged.
  • Legal requirements: we disclose information if required by law, and we will notify you where the law allows.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your personal records, your share grants, and — if you are the last member — your household, its kitchen and its receipt photos, normally within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something longer. Your rights

You can access, export, correct or delete your information, withdraw consent, and ask questions about how it is handled. Most of this can be done in the app; otherwise contact us. We respond within 30 days. In Québec you may also request information about automated processing and data portability. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Québec, the Commission d'accès à l'information.

Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, strict access rules tested automatically, app integrity checks, per-user limits on AI requests, and audit logs for sharing. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the regulators as required by law.

Children

Chefii is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Not medical advice

Nutrition values, freshness dates and health notes in Chefii are estimates and general information. Chefii does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
