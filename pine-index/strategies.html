One release

AURUM-NY PRIME

A New-York-open confluence engine for gold, built to an FTMO risk envelope. Long only, one position at a time, non-repainting by construction. The Pine file is the instrument; the statistics it cannot compute honestly are exported and run through a Python validation suite.

XAUUSD5m executionLong onlyPine v6139 inputs

In an established bullish gold regime, when Asia→London structure agrees, macro and DXY support gold, the NY opening range shows bullish acceptance, price holds above a rising NY-anchored VWAP, and two confirmed higher lows define ascending support — a third rejection off that support plus a break of short-term microstructure is a better long than a plain opening breakout.

How it behaves

  • FTMO risk module: daily-loss and total-loss limits, trading schedule, kill switch, pass-path simulation.
  • Spread is a modelled cost, labelled as such — Pine has no bid/ask, and the file says so rather than pretending.
  • News blackouts come from user-supplied timestamps; the dashboard reports how many were supplied.
  • 43-column per-position trade export feeds validation/aurum_validate.py for bootstrapped expectancy, block-bootstrap Monte Carlo and ablation tables.
  • Ships with ten spec documents (S1–S90 markers in the source map to the maths spec).

Where it stands

Code complete. Waiting on your call: run the v7 four-family engine, or revert to the v6 baseline trendline setup for the backtest protocol.

Three generations

CYBER MCGIVER

Multi-timeframe trend plus confirmed-trendline retest on gold. The lineage is the story: v1/v5 defined the setup, v6 added controlled experiments around it, and v7 tore up the architecture after the AND-chain of ten vetoes produced eleven trades in ten years.

XAUUSD15m4 setup familiesPine v6145 inputs

Stop vetoing. Four setup families run in parallel and each emits candidates; every candidate enters one shared pipeline — score, then risk, then reward-room, then execution. Only risk conditions still veto.

How it behaves

  • Setup families: trendline third-touch continuation (the v6 idea, preserved), liquidity sweep + reclaim, breakout + retest, and VWAP/trend-continuation pullback.
  • Non-safety conditions contribute to a transparent 0–100 setup score from ten weighted components; every weight is an input, and the score is normalised by the weight sum so re-weighting doesn't silently move tier boundaries.
  • Mandatory vetoes are risk-only: a valid structural stop, stop no wider than N ATR, enough reward room, a fundable size, available margin, the daily trade cap.
  • Reference run over ten years of XAUUSD-like 15m bars: 3,193 trades vs v6's 11 — 1.22 per trading day, 69.1% of days with at least one trade, 5.95 candidates offered per day.
  • Score floor stays at 72 even though 70 would hit the 1.3–1.8/day target. Moving it is a decision, not a bug fix.

Where it stands

Reference run documented. Zero-trade days sit at 30.9% against a 15–20% guideline — the report attributes it to a long-only engine on days the higher timeframes point down.

XAUUSD15m4 research profilesPine v674 inputs

The v5 setup with two evidence-driven experiments bolted on, without removing the baseline — so the comparison stays controlled.

How it behaves

  • Experiment A — trendline quality band: preferred normalized slope 0.075–0.150 ATR/bar.
  • Experiment B — failure-to-follow-through protection: no +0.25R MFE within three completed 15m bars after fill requests a market close.
  • The Research profile input switches between four controlled runs: CONTROL (v5 baseline), A (slope only), B (follow-through only), C (both — the default candidate).
  • Kept as baseline/ next to v7, untouched, so v7's frequency claims have something to be measured against.

Where it stands

Superseded by v7, retained deliberately as the comparison baseline. This is the file that produced 11 trades in 10 years.

XAUUSDMTF 1D·4H·1HPine v672 inputs

Trade only when 1D, 4H and 1H agree; wait for an execution-timeframe break of structure; build a trendline from P1 and P2; require P3 to confirm it; take touch #4 after a rejection candle and a break of that candle's extreme.

How it behaves

  • Stop beyond P3; size so the planned loss is ≤1% of current equity; 50% off at +1.2R; runner protected at cost-adjusted breakeven; trail to a 10R ceiling.
  • Session is an analytical label only — the engine deliberately does not filter by session.
  • Costs (spread, slippage, commission) are modelled and feed sizing and breakeven; the dashboard flags mismatches against the backtester's own declaration-time constants.
  • Deliberately not optimised — the file calls itself a measurement instrument, and S1–S75 markers map to the specification document.

Where it stands

The original. Referenced in later sessions as the “v5 baseline”; a diagnostic run flagged the funnel dying before entry, which is what motivated v6 and then v7.

Two revisions

XAUUSD 08:30 NY ORB

The opening-range breakout that must trade every day. v1 was audited against the requirement that decisions happen on 1-minute bars; it failed that test outright, and v2 is the rewrite.

XAUUSD1-minute1 trade/day2R target22 inputs

Same opening-range idea, decided on the timeframe you actually asked for. Runs on the chart's own timeframe and refuses only timeframes too coarse to resolve a five-minute range.

How it behaves

  • Forced entry moved to the window's last bar (08:59). On 1-minute bars, v1's 08:55 threw away the final four minutes of the search.
  • Safety net on the first bar after the window closes, so one missing bar can't cost the day its trade.
  • Stop-distance floor (min_stop_atr, default 0.5 ATR) — a tight 1m interaction candle otherwise gives near-zero risk and a position sized to match.
  • Notional capped at a multiple of equity (default 5×). Unfloored risk sizing asked for $13.0M of gold against a $100k account in the lab.
  • Confirmation may arrive within N bars of the interaction (max_bars_since_touch, default 2; set 1 for v1's behaviour).
  • The “XAU in the ticker” check is now a dashboard warning, not a silent trading gate — a broker feed named GOLD used to trade nothing, with no visible reason.

Where it stands

Delivered as a .pine file. Checked against a Python re-implementation (src/invest/strategies/orb.py) with 32 behavioural tests over constructed bars.

XAUUSD5-minute only1 trade/day2R target18 inputs

08:30–08:35 ET builds the range; 08:35–09:00 hunts the first confirmed penetration; a deterministic fallback at the end of the window guarantees one order per eligible day.

How it behaves

  • Hard-gated to 5-minute charts: tfOK = timeframe.in_seconds(timeframe.period) == 300, ANDed into every signal, every interaction record and the forced entry. On a 1m chart it takes zero trades, every day, forever.
  • Kept unmodified for diffing against v2 — this is the submitted original, not a patched copy.
  • What was already right and survived into v2: a bar can never confirm itself, the structural stop under the interaction wick, the exact-R target, one entry per NY day, and the end-of-day flat.

Where it stands

Archived reference. Superseded by v2; kept so the seven audit findings stay checkable.
